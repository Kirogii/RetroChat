# Emoji artwork

Twemoji artwork: Copyright Twitter, Inc. and other contributors.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International; see LICENSE-GRAPHICS.
Upstream: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji

These PNG assets were exported from the COLR/CPAL artwork in the installed Roblox
TwemojiMozilla.ttf font (client version-93202a13414c4131) and rasterized to 72x72.
The extraction scripts are in tools/extract_emoji.py and tools/rasterize_emoji.cjs.
Glyph shapes and colour layers are preserved; the packaging changes vectors to PNG.

Emoji aliases are from https://github.com/github/gemoji; see LICENSE.
